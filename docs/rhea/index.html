FIVE DIFFERENT JOBS.

The projects are deliberately not one monolith. Storage authority, invariant admission, operational knowledge, network mutation and architecture composition remain separate so one receipt cannot silently certify another domain.

Rhea preserves succession. The stone deceives Cronus. The cave protects the child. The Kouretes coordinate under noise. Blueshoes runs for open terrain. The myth is a mnemonic—not an excuse to skip the tests.

RHEA PROJECT / ῬΕΑ

Proof before birth.

Active v2 workstream: staged authority boundaries, independently verified transitions, and a first bounded immutable-byte contract. Current v2 status is DESIGN_ONLY; Stage 01 contracts are the admitted work.

Rhea, mother of the Olympian succession: the future survives because the current ruler does not get to consume every possible next state.

Proposal ≠ capability. Capability ≠ execution. Execution ≠ evidence until a qualified observer can check it.

RHEKNEL / INVARIANT GATE

The stone Cronus cannot digest.

A small C prototype of tagged judge/action dispatch with bounded registries and three verdicts: OK, REJECT, ESCALATE. Not a certified safety kernel; the included string judge is a demonstration heuristic.

Rhea hands Cronus a wrapped stone instead of Zeus. Here the stone is the categorical NO that persuasive probabilistic machinery cannot negotiate away.

Deterministic boundary first. Grand safety claims only after executable receipts.
ENTER RHEKNEL ↗
OMNIA VAULT / IMMUTABLE STATE

The cave remembers.

Prototype SQLite/Node and Rust supervisor data paths exist. The target is content-addressed immutable objects, causal revisions, conditional heads, atomic publication and safe native projections. The current checkout is not yet that finished sync engine.

The Cretan cave hides what must survive. The outside world may forget, crash, disconnect or disagree; the protected lineage still has an identity.

Conflict is data. Silent overwrite is data loss.

OMNIA PLAYBOOK / OPERATIONAL CORPUS

Dance the checks first.

Vendor-neutral invariants, adapters, read-only diagnostics, playbooks and generated reports. Current commands deliberately do not mutate DNS, firewall, credentials, packages or networking.

The Kouretes' shield-dance hides Zeus's cries in coordinated noise. The engineering version is choreography: know which invariant, check and procedure belongs where before touching the machine.

A diagnostic that secretly mutates reality is an executor wearing a fake moustache.
ENTER THE PLAYBOOK ↗
BLUESHOES / FLOW SURGERY

Paths are negotiable.

Post-OpenWrt routing research centered on Flows and Flow Surgery: preserve the boundary conditions of useful flow while routes, names, media and cryptographic epochs may mutate under explicit authority.

Not an Olympian office. More like leaving the palace entirely: if the road becomes the cage, stop worshipping the road.

Flows survive. Paths mutate. The public site is a lighthouse, not a routing root.

PEDANTRY / IMPORTANT

Κρόνος ≠ Χρόνος.

Cronus the Titan and Chronos the personification of time are distinct figures despite later conflations. The architecture uses the former myth; it does not build technical claims on the confusion.

Even mythology deserves type safety.

Likewise, the poetic association of Rhea with rheō, “to flow,” is useful wordplay but not treated here as a settled etymological proof.
