NO MAGIC.

Ruliada names the creative question: which other states could be possible? Aletheia names the demand to bring a claim and its evidence into view. The Rhea repository carries related research and proof-oriented experiments. These names are not new shipped engines, a universal truth oracle, or evidence of a Ricci-flow router.

A thousand agents agreeing is still an observation about agents. A measured bottleneck is evidence about a path. Neither gets to masquerade as the other.
