The direction.

An OpenBSD-first, RAM-only router OS. Local authority. Bounded changes. Recovery by design.

Production Flow Surgery, Rheknel admission, RAM-only boot, and post-quantum negotiation remain things to prove. Ambition is welcome. Borrowed certainty is not.
